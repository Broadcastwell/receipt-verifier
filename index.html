      What the checks mean
      
    
      A receipt is VERIFIED when the record exists at its public source, the SHA-256 of the full answer text as published equals the receipt's hash, and no check fails. It is FAILED when any field disagrees with the source, and INCOMPLETE when a source could not be read. A check that cannot be made says so, with its reason, and never counts as a failure.
      
    
      Some sources do not allow a browser on another site to read them: the fictional sample export and the Decision Receipt links. Here those checks show NOT CHECKABLE. The command line reads them:
      
    
      npx github:Broadcastwell/receipt-verifier "crm|1|ChatGPT|1"
      
    
      The Absence Index measures answers from ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode. The dataset is published under CC BY 4.0 with DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22907695.
